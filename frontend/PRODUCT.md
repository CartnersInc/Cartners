# Cartners

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Families, roommates, and friends coordinating meals and groceries. New visitors need to establish their own household and identity before using shared data.

## Product Purpose

Turn a meal idea or grocery request into a reviewed shared list, a coordinated store run, and an optional record of actual spending. Reduce the coordination work usually carried by one person.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/Vite frontend, FastAPI backend, SQLite storage. Preserve onboarding, household membership, Gemini meal and grocery drafts, refinements, recipe discovery carousel, dated plans, food preferences, shared/personal shopping requests, purchasing status, list clearing and Undo, Kroger comparison, store runs, receipt drafts, reviewed expenses, and household invitations. AI outputs require review. Store estimates, sample data and real provider prices must remain distinguishable. No invented pantry, prices, transactions or account security claims.

## Brand Commitments

Name and lowercase wordmark: cartners. The user explicitly selected [DubHacks 2026](https://dh26.dubhacks.co/) as the visual and UX reference after rejecting the earlier purple/yellow palette and engraved ingredient illustration. The replacement uses cream, cocoa, warm blue, parchment and flat playful kitchen-object art. Preserve every existing feature and real recipe photography. All five navigation destinations must remain visible on desktop and mobile. Cartners is a functional product rather than a sponsor-branded site; no affiliation is implied.

The Cook Smarter track guides utility: reduce friction and household coordination through practical AI automation, visible progress, continuous interaction states and efficient review. Movement should explain changes without discarding entered work or blocking controls.

## Product Principles

- One primary next action per decision.
- Carry existing information forward instead of repeating forms.
- Make the interface useful with or without AI and preserve input on failure.
- People confirm purchases, spending, restrictions and shared changes.

## Evidence on Hand

Current frontend and backend, ../docs/PRD.md, ../docs/API_CONTRACT.md and fictional ../docs/fixtures. The user supplied Cook Smarter and kitchen-themed track language and pinned the live DubHacks 2026 site as the replacement reference. Original flat illustration assets and motion implementations are present in the frontend. No official DubHacks art or permission to imply an affiliation is supplied.

## Accessibility

Labeled keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, at least 44px primary touch controls, layouts without phone overflow, and reduced-motion support.
